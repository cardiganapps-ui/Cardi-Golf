**What I ran.** Everything is in `$S/panel/evidence/PERF/`, and each timing file records `/proc/loadavg`.

- **Bundle.** `npx vite build --sourcemap` with `.env.example` into `dist/` (scratch). `attribute.mjs` (source-map-js) sums the generated bytes per original source and per package. The result was checked against production's served file (curl: raw, gzip, br, zstd and cache headers).
- **Lighthouse 12.8.2.** I reused the chair's three simulated reports and added five runs with applied DevTools throttling (prod `/` ×2, the fixture on my server ×2, prod `/t/ensayo` ×1). `lh-summary.mjs` extracts LCP phases, requests, bootup and unused code.
- **Engine.** Scratch vitest config rooted at the repo (`vitest.bench.config.mjs`, `bench/engine.bench.test.ts`). Four repetitions: one at load ≈4 before the restart and three at load 0.5–2.3 after it. A CPU profile through `node:inspector`.
- **Instrumented builds.** `vite.perf.config.ts` is the repo's config plus a plugin that assigns the store, `dataFromSnapshot` and the outbox to `globalThis`, in a minified and an unminified variant. They were served on :4186 only; no repo file was changed.
- **Re-renders.** A `__REACT_DEVTOOLS_GLOBAL_HOOK__` stub implementing DevTools' `didFiberRender` (the PerformedWork flag; it descends only where the child list changed), a MutationObserver, and CDP `Performance.getMetrics` deltas (`render-live.mjs`, `perf-lib.mjs`). 4× CPU runs repeated three times.
- **Interactions.** Trusted Playwright clicks with Event Timing (`durationThreshold 16`) and a MutationObserver on the hole number (`tarjeta.mjs`: 6 stepper taps and 5 saves per fixture), tab switches (`tabs.mjs`), and the TV rotation (`tv.mjs`).
- **Production Ensayo** (`ensayo-net.mjs`). One Nico session: one anonymous sign-in and PIN 1234 entered once, saved to my own state file. The warm-reopen waterfall ran under CDP network throttling and 4× CPU. Five reloads were triggered with `visibilitychange`, the same `reload()` a Realtime event calls. **No writes to Ensayo**: no scores, no status changes.
- **Realtime idle** (`rt-idle.mjs`). Node with supabase-js on an undici transport through the egress proxy, using the app's exact 20-binding channel and Nico's JWT, for 130 s.
- **Memory** (`memory.mjs`). Two 24-minute runs: production `/t/ensayo/tv` reloading every 20 s, and fixture large60 En vivo with a synthetic score change every 10 s. `HeapProfiler.collectGarbage` before each 60 s sample.
- **Service worker install** (`sw-install.mjs`): a fresh visitor on production `/`, no sign-in. **Share** (`share.mjs`): first and second taps.

**What I could not check, and why.**
- **No real devices.** There is no Android phone or iPhone here, so 4× throttling on a server core stands in for a mid-range phone; real phones with small caches may do worse on the GC-heavy recomputes.
- **Realtime inside Chromium.** The sandbox relay rejects Chromium's WebSocket handshake to Supabase (REL's finding). The reload cost was therefore measured through the identical `visibilitychange` path, and the double reload on foreground comes from code reading.
- **Energy.** No battery or radio-energy measurement is possible in a container. Battery statements are derived from request counts, radio-active time and CPU time.
- **Contention.** About 20 agents share 4 CPUs. Applied-throttling Lighthouse runs after 20:06 ran at load 4.5–8, which inflates their TBT and TTI; the medians and loads are given, and network timings are unaffected.
- **Server side.** No Supabase metrics or Realtime per-subscriber cost (no PAT or service key), and no Vercel analytics (read-only connector, and no analytics enabled).
- **Paid options.** Supabase image transformations (paid) were not tried.
