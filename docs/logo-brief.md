# Logo brief: Polo

A brief for a designer. Everything they need to know about the product to draw its mark; nothing about the code.

## The product in one paragraph

Polo is a web app (installable on the phone) for running amateur golf tournaments among friends. An organizer creates a tournament, sets its rules and side games, and shares a code. Players join on their phones, enter their strokes hole by hole on the course, and everyone sees live leaderboards, side games (pairs, "the snake" for three-putts, fewest putts), a Calcutta auction at the dinner the night before, and exactly who owes whom at the end. A TV mode shows the boards at the villa and at the ceremony. Real money moves between friends based on these numbers, so the product has to feel **trustworthy and exact** before it feels fun.

The first tournament is a 12-player bachelor trip in Los Cabos (April 2027), but the product is a platform: every tournament is its own event with its own name, logo and accent color. **This brief is for the platform's logo, not for any one tournament's.**

## Who sees it

- Friends in their 30s, mostly in Mexico, who play weekend golf. Not pros, not a country club board.
- On a phone home screen, in bright sun, next to WhatsApp and banking apps.
- In the browser tab (16 px), in the app header beside the tournament's name, on share images sent to WhatsApp groups, and small in the footer of the TV board.

## The visual world it has to live in

The app's design direction is called **"La tarjeta"**: the paper scorecard and the pencil. The interface looks like a well-printed club scorecard: near-white card stock, graphite ink, a ruled grid, tabular figures that line up. No cream, no gradients, no cards-and-shadows, no emoji.

The one signature detail is **golf's pencil notation** on the scorecard: a hand-drawn **circle around a score means birdie**, a double circle an eagle, a square a bogey. Every golfer reads it instantly. It appears across the app, and the current placeholder icon is a geometric "P" with a red pencil circle around it (it reads too much like a parking sign, which is one reason we need you). You're welcome to beat that idea, but the logo should feel like it belongs to that same scorecard-and-pencil world.

**Palette the app already uses** (the logo should work with these; it doesn't have to use all of them):

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Card stock (background) | `#FBFAF7` |
| Graphite ink | `#1B211D` |
| Fairway green (the one accent) | `#1E6B3B` |
| Under-par red (the pencil circle) | `#9D1C23` |
| Over-par blue | `#235380` |
| Board surface (TV, dark) | `#0F2E22` with `#F6F3EA` figures and `#F2C230` for the leader |

**Type in the app:** Archivo (a sturdy grotesque with a width axis) for everything, and Fraunces (a serif) only for each tournament's name. A wordmark can be custom lettering or built on one of these, but it should sit comfortably beside a Fraunces event name without competing with it.

## Personality

- **Exact, calm, a little dry.** Like a good scorecard or a well-kept ledger. Not loud, not jokey.
- **Golf-literate.** Should feel made by someone who plays, not by someone who searched "golf icon".
- **Among friends.** Warm enough for a bachelor trip; not corporate, not a country-club crest.
- **Quiet chrome, loud events.** The platform mark steps back so each tournament's own name and logo can be the star.

## Avoid

- Golf clichés: crossed clubs, a ball on a tee, a flagstick with a waving flag, a swinging silhouette, a golf ball with dimples as the main idea.
- Crests, shields, laurels, faux-heraldry.
- **The other polos.** The name is "Polo" but the app is about golf: no horses, mallets or riders (the sport), no shirt collars or anything that echoes Ralph Lauren's polo player or other polo-shirt brands, no ice pops, no Marco Polo. It must read as golf, not as any of those.
- A **P inside a circle**: that is a parking sign.
- Anything that reads as a clothing brand.
- Gradients, glossy effects, drop shadows, 3D.
- Anything that only works in color or only works large.

## Deliverables

1. **Symbol / app icon.** Must read at 16 px (favicon) and 29 px (iOS settings), and look finished at 180 px (iOS home screen) and 512 px (Android). Provide a version on a full-bleed square (the OS applies its own rounded mask; keep the mark inside the central 80% "safe zone" for Android maskable icons).
2. **Wordmark** "Polo" (capital P, the rest lowercase), horizontal.
3. **Lockup** of symbol + wordmark.
4. Each in: full color on card stock, one-color graphite, and reversed on the dark board surface `#0F2E22`.
5. **Files:** SVG (outlined, no live text) for everything; PNG exports of the icon at 16, 32, 180, 192, 512 px and a 512 px maskable version.
6. A one-page sheet showing minimum size, clear space, and the do/don't.

## How we'll judge it

- Recognizable at 16 px in a browser tab and on a crowded phone home screen.
- A golfer gets "golf" from it without a single cliché.
- Looks right next to the app's scorecard UI and next to a tournament's own logo (the first one is an embroidered patch; others will vary) without fighting either.
- Works in one color, on the dark TV board, and printed on a paper scorecard.
